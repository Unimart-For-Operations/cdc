# refactor(ci): use if-guards for GitHub-only workflows instead of file move

Replaces the physical file-move approach to GitHub-only workflows with
job-level if-guards. Files stay in the standard .github/workflows/ path
where they are discoverable and self-documenting while being
automatically skipped on Gitea Actions.
