# fix(publish): fix 5 SetRemoteAndPush bugs, add Gitea org management

The publish step in unimart open was silently failing due to 5 bugs
in SetRemoteAndPush — wrong working directory, no TLS bypass, no
auth credentials, destructive remote overwrite, and hooks running on
mirror pushes. All fixed. Additionally, repos now publish under a
proper "idpbuilder" org in Gitea (with auto-creation) instead of
under the giteaAdmin user, and the meta repo itself is included.
